Assignment 1 Reflection

1. The easiest part of this assignment was setting up the three HTML files and adding the basic structure with the html, head and body tags. Once I understood where to put the title and the content, creating the index page with headings, paragraphs and links felt straightforward.

2. The most challenging part was recreating the Punk Planet page as HTML. It was difficult to decide which parts should become headings, paragraphs or lists, and I sometimes had to experiment with the order of the elements to make the page look similar to the original. I also had to pay attention to indentation and closing tags so that the browser showed everything correctly.

3. Some details of the magazine page were not possible for me to reproduce in pure HTML at this stage. I could not copy the exact fonts, colours and layout, and complex visual elements such as background graphics or precise column positions. For this assignment I focused on recreating the text content and the basic structure instead of the exact design.
